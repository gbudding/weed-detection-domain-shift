## Sources

- Sudars et al. (2020), *Data in Brief* 31:105833, doi:10.1016/j.dib.2020.105833. Greenhouse: Canon EOS 800D, plants in pots (peat) under natural + LED light, Jelgava. Field: Intel RealSense D435 and Sony W800 on a wheeled platform, organic farms in Latvia. The paper reports 1118 images; the Mendeley release (v1, doi:10.17632/nj4vtk4tt6.1, CC BY 4.0) reports 1176. Both report 7853 boxes, which matches our count (7442 weed + 411 crop).
- No image has EXIF metadata, so the camera can't be read from the files. Domain is inferred from filename, size and content.

- **greenhouse**: orange planters, peat, strong magenta/yellow LED casts. Mixed sizes (1000×750, 640×480, 640×360, 480×384, plus portrait versions). Canon naming (`IMG_`) matches the paper's greenhouse camera.
- **field**: bare, cracked soil, all 1280×720 (a RealSense resolution). Low saturation (mean HSV S ≈ 24 vs 110 in the greenhouse).
- **uncertain**: 1280×720 like the field set, but peat substrate with black pot edges and many near-duplicate frames. Looks like RealSense in a controlled setting. Excluded by default.

## Findings that affect the design

1. **The `crop` class barely exists in the field** (7 boxes in 908 images), so field crop AP can't be measured.
2. **Field crops appear to be unannotated.** Field images often show large crop plants (broad leaves) with no box. Only small weeds are boxed. On the field test set, any `crop` prediction, and possibly predictions on large plants, would count as false positives even when correct. This affects the error analysis ("background FP" will include unlabelled plants).
   The greenhouse set has the same issue to a lesser degree. In `yolo_check.jpg`, rows of beet and carrot seedlings are unboxed while the weeds beside them are boxed. Crop labelling is partial in both domains, so crop AP (even in-domain) should be treated with caution.
3. **The source domain is small**: 241 greenhouse images to split into train/val/test, against a 908-image field test set.
4. **Objects are tiny.** At imgsz 640 the median box side is 15 px (field) and 19 px (greenhouse). 3253 of 5973 field boxes are under 16 px. Resolution is likely to matter a lot, which supports the resolution ablation.
5. **Near-duplicates / leakage.**
   - Field: 108 consecutive-ID pairs are near-identical (video-like sequences, about 72 runs).
   - Greenhouse: few pixel-level duplicates, but the same planters are photographed repeatedly (IDs 5910–6198).
   - Splits should group by sequence/planter, not by random image.
6. Checks: every image has an XML with matching filename and size. There are no out-of-bounds boxes, no EXIF rotation, and only the `weed` and `crop` classes. One degenerate box (zero height) exists: `IMG_5967`, crop, (44,377)–(45,377). The converter must drop it.
